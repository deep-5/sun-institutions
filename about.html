About SUN Group of Institutions

Founded on the pillars of academic rigor, character building, and social responsibility, SUN Group has spent over two decades molding high-achieving leaders across science, healthcare, and sustainable agriculture.

OUR VISION

Pioneering Holistic Education

To be an internationally recognized center of academic distinction that nurtures innovative intellects, ethical healthcare providers, technological leaders, and agrarian scientists dedicated to societal upliftment and sustainable development.

OUR MISSION

Empowering Every Student

To deliver accessible, experiential, and research-infused education through state-of-the-art laboratory infrastructure, university-affiliated curricula, industry internships, and committed faculty mentorship.

GOVERNANCE & LEADERSHIP

Educational Trust & Governing Council

The SUN Educational Trust comprises eminent academicians, medical directors, agricultural scientists, and philanthropic leaders who steer institutional policy, infrastructure development, and quality assurance.
