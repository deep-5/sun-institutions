Research & Innovation Hub

Driving discovery and pragmatic solutions in organic agronomy, crop resilience, biomechanical therapies, and diagnostic biotechnology.

Agri-Innovation

50-Acre Live Experimental Farm

Dedicated agricultural research station testing organic cultivation techniques, micro-irrigation efficiency, seed pathology, and climate-resilient crop varieties.

Clinical Trials

Biomechanics & Neuro-Rehabilitation

Investigating advanced therapeutic exercise modalities, spinal injury rehabilitation, robotic gait training, and pediatric neuromuscular physiotherapy.

Publications

Central Research Journal Repository

Access to 5,000+ indexed international journals, IEEE papers, UGC-CARE list articles, and faculty-guided undergraduate research monographs.

FACILITIES & INFRASTRUCTURE

Specialized Research Laboratories

Soil & Seed Pathology Testing Station

Equipped with atomic absorption spectroscopy, soil moisture sensors, and germination incubator chambers.

Biochemical Diagnostic Research Suite

Fully automated biochemistry analyzers, spectrophotometers, and cell culture laminar flow hoods.

Advanced Computing & AI Sandbox

High-performance workstation clusters for machine learning, data mining, and statistical simulation.

Clinical Motion & Ergonomics Center

Force plates, EMG telemetry, and digital gait tracking tools for rehabilitation analysis.
