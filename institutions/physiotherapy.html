Sun College of Physiotherapy

Preparing compassionate, clinically skilled physiotherapy professionals through advanced biomechanics labs, hospital clinical postings, and evidence-based rehabilitation protocols.

CLINICAL EXCELLENCE

Hands-on Patient Rehabilitation & Therapy

Sun College of Physiotherapy is affiliated to Dr. NTR University of Health Sciences, Vijayawada and recognized by the Government of Andhra Pradesh. The college focuses on comprehensive theoretical foundations in Anatomy, Physiology, Biomechanics, Exercise Therapy, and Electrotherapy combined with extensive daily clinical rotations.

Our students receive intensive training at affiliated super-specialty hospitals in Visakhapatnam, managing neurology rehabilitation, orthopedic post-op mobilization, pediatric therapy, and sports injury recovery under senior physical therapists.

Offered Physiotherapy Programs

BPT (Bachelor of Physiotherapy)

4.5 Years Degree

4-year classroom and clinical curriculum plus mandatory 6-month rotatory internship at multi-specialty hospitals. Full exposure to ICU chest physiotherapy, orthopedic manual therapy and stroke rehabilitation.

Eligibility: 10+2 Intermediate with BiPC min 50% aggregate

MPT (Master of Physiotherapy)

2 Years PG

Postgraduate specializations in Musculoskeletal/Orthopedics, Neurosciences, Cardiovascular & Pulmonary Rehabilitation, and Sports Physiotherapy.

Eligibility: BPT Degree with completed 6-month internship

Admissions & Clinical Tie-ups

  • Affiliation: Dr. NTR UHS, Vijayawada
  • Clinical Beds: 500+ Bed Hospital Tie-ups
  • Internship: Mandatory 6 Months Paid
  • Placement: Apollo, Care, KIMS Hospitals
