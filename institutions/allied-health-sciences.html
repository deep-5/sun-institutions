SUN Institute of Allied Health Sciences

Empowering the backbone of healthcare with high-caliber technical education in medical diagnostics, radiology imaging, laboratory analysis, and emergency surgical support.

HEALTHCARE DIAGNOSTICS

Essential Paramedical Disciplines for Modern Hospitals

Modern medicine relies heavily on skilled paramedical and allied health specialists. SUN Institute of Allied Health Sciences (SIAHS) provides rigorous academic instruction and practical diagnostic competencies in biochemistry, hematology, microbiology, pathology, radiography, and surgical suite management.

Students operate high-end diagnostic analyzers, MRI/CT consoles, and surgical sterilization equipment during regular clinical shifts at our partner hospitals.

Offered Paramedical Programs

B.Sc Medical Laboratory Technology (BMLT)

3 Years

Clinical biochemistry, medical hematology, parasitology, diagnostic microbiology, histology and blood banking safety.

Eligibility: 10+2 Intermediate with BiPC / MLT Vocational

B.Sc Radiography & Imaging Technology

3 Years

Digital X-Ray imaging, Computed Tomography (CT), Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI), ultrasound imaging physics and radiation safety protocols.

Eligibility: 10+2 Intermediate with Science stream

B.Sc Operation Theatre & Anesthesia Technology

3 Years

Surgical asepsis, anesthesia workstations, patient monitoring, surgical instrumentation and intensive care unit (ICU) management.

Eligibility: 10+2 Intermediate with Science stream

Diploma in Medical Laboratory Technology (DMLT)

2 Years

Practical laboratory diagnostics, specimen collection, microscope maintenance, routine urine and blood pathology.

Eligibility: 10th / 10+2 Pass

Admissions & Key Facts

  • Approval: AP Paramedical Board
  • Diagnostic Labs: 4 Fully Automated Labs
  • Postings: Daily Hospital Shifts
  • Placements: 100% Diagnostic Centers
